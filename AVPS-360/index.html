THE TASK

Task definition

Audio-visual instance segmentation (AVIS) segments and tracks individual sound-producing objects in videos. Models must segment visible persons while they produce sound, suppress their masks during silence even when they remain visible, and preserve their identities when sound production resumes.

01

Sound-producing persons

Each visible sound-producing person receives one mask per timestamp and retains the same identity across sound-event classes; simultaneous event labels are allowed.

02

Panoramic instances

Regions of the same person split across the panoramic seam are annotated as a single instance mask.

03

Recording-level identities

A person track links the annotated masks of the same person throughout one recording.

THE DATASET

The AVPS-360 dataset

AVPS-360 is built on STARSS23, a dataset of indoor sound scenes improvised by participants following general instructions and recorded in 23 rooms with a spherical microphone array and a co-located 360° camera. It provides synchronised four-channel first-order Ambisonics, equirectangular video at 1920 × 960 pixels and 29.97 frames per second, and spatiotemporal sound-event annotations.

The dataset captures intermittent sound production in real-world interactions dominated by speech, alongside laughter, clapping, and footsteps.

We keep the STARSS23 partition: its development set becomes our training split and its evaluation set our test split, so no room or recording session is shared between the two.
